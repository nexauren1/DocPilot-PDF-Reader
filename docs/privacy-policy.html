DocPilot Privacy Policy

PDF reading and document tools for Android

Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Overview

DocPilot is an Android PDF reader and document toolkit. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. You can read and use local PDF tools without creating an account.

2. Documents and files

PDFs and images that you select are opened and processed on your device by DocPilot. DocPilot does not operate a document-upload server and does not intentionally send your document contents, extracted text, or filenames to the app developer.

The app may ask you to choose individual files through Android's document picker. If you choose to grant broad storage access, the app can search for PDFs in shared storage to populate your library. Your library, favourites, reading position, and reader preferences are stored locally on your device.

3. Accounts and authentication

Creating an account is optional. If you choose email/password or Google sign-in, the authentication provider processes the account information needed to create and authenticate your account. DocPilot does not need an account to open or process local PDFs.

For information about Google's handling of information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and advertising

DocPilot does not include Google Analytics or in-app advertising in this release. The app does not send document names or PDF contents to an analytics service.

5. Scanner and text recognition

When you choose scanning or OCR features, the app may use Google Play services and ML Kit components to provide those features. Their availability and processing behaviour can depend on the Android device and Google service implementation. Review Google's privacy information for details about its services. DocPilot does not receive a copy of your scanned document through a DocPilot-operated upload service.

6. Permissions and security

Storage access is used to locate, read, and save documents when you request those actions. You can use the system file picker for individual files instead of granting broad storage access. Keep your device protected, and avoid sharing files containing information you do not want others to see.

7. Retention and deletion

Local library entries, favourites, and reader settings remain on your device until you remove them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. If you created an optional account and want to request account deletion, contact the developer through the support link below. Account deletion requests may require enough information to identify the account, but never send passwords or sign-in codes.

8. Children and privacy choices

DocPilot is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed to collect information from children. You can decline optional account sign-in and use the local reading features without an account.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision. The published policy should be reviewed again before each major release.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact the developer through the DocPilot project support page.

This policy is a product disclosure, not legal advice. The app listing and this policy must match the actual SDKs and behaviour included in the published build.
